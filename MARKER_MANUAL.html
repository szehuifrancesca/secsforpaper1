1. Quick Launch & Authentication

The School-Based Exam Control System (SECS) runs directly in your web browser (Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome). It requires no internet connectivity and operates completely offline on your school-issued laptop.

[1] Double-click "start_secs.bat" ──► [2] Enter Marker Passcode (Default: 1132)
[3] Select Target Exam Paper (e.g. 2026 P3 EOY Paper 1) ──► [4] Select Marker Code (e.g. M06)
[5] (If KP Moderator selected) Enter Confidential KP PIN ──► [6] Arrive at Marker Portal

1.1 Step-by-Step Login

  1. Navigate to the folder on your laptop or shared drive: school-exam-control-system.
  2. Double-click start_secs.bat (or right-click index.html → Open with Microsoft Edge / Google Chrome).
  3. On the initial security prompt, enter the Paper Marker Passcode (default: 1132 or the code communicated by your HOD).
  4. Click Unlock Portal.
  5. From the dropdowns, select:
    • Examination Paper: Choose the specific paper you are grading (e.g., 2026 P3 EOY Paper 1 or 2026 P5 EOY Paper 1).
    • Marker Code & Name: Choose your assigned marker identity (e.g., M06 - Mdm Tan).
  6. Click Start Marking. You will enter the streamlined Marker Portal.

2. Linking the School Shared Network Folder

To ensure that your marked scripts, tallies, and class completion rates synchronize smoothly with your peer markers and HOD in real time without lag:

📁 1-Click Shared Folder Link:
  1. At the top-right header, click the folder badge "📁 Link School Shared Folder".
  2. In the browser file picker, select your school's designated shared exam folder: EXAM DATA FOLDER (LINK TO THIS FOLDER).
  3. Click "Select Folder" and grant "View and edit files" permission.
  4. The badge immediately turns green: 🟢 Linked: EXAM DATA FOLDER.

Once connected:

  • Zero-Collision Partitioning: Your marked scripts are written to dedicated, paper-specific files (e.g. marker_exam_1791451628276_M06.json). No marker ever writes to another marker's file, preventing file lock conflicts.
  • Zero-Lag Keystroke Protection: The sync engine automatically suspends background checks while you are actively keying marks, ensuring that typing and cursor movement remain 100% fluid.
  • Real-Time Tally Broadcasting: As soon as you click Save on a script, your progress counters (Cohort Marked, Class Marked, and My Marked) update immediately and broadcast to the HOD live dashboard.
💡 Opening SECS on Another Laptop? Check These 2 Steps:
  1. Same Shared Folder Location: Ensure that both laptops select the exact same folder on your school network drive (e.g. S:\Exam) or synced OneDrive folder. If Laptop 1 and Laptop 2 select different folders on their local C: drives, they will not see each other's data.
  2. Connection Gate Permission: Because web browsers do not allow silent background disk access across machines, you must click "Select School Shared Folder" when prompted upon login on each laptop. Once linked, all candidate rosters, live marks, and progress bars synchronize automatically within 3.5 seconds.

3. Platinum Standard Rubric Isolation (No Overwriting Across Papers)

To guarantee complete credibility and eliminate any confusion of marks when markers grade multiple cohorts (e.g., P3 and P5), the system enforces strict boundaries at all levels:

Cohort / Level Content Rubric Max Language Rubric Max Total Paper 1 Max
Primary 3 (P3) Max 7 (0 to 7) Max 8 (0 to 8) Max 15 Marks
Primary 4 (P4) Max 7 (0 to 7) Max 8 (0 to 8) Max 15 Marks
Primary 5 (P5) Max 20 (0 to 20) Max 20 (0 to 20) Max 40 Marks
Primary 5 HCL (Higher MT) Max 20 (0 to 20) Max 20 (0 to 20) Max 40 Marks
🛡️ Platinum Boundary Protection Rules:
  • Independent File Partitioning: P3 grading files and P5 grading files are completely separate on disk. Switching papers never touches or overwrites another paper's data.
  • Input Guard: When marking P3, the system strictly blocks any Content mark greater than 7 or Language mark greater than 8. If an accidental keystroke exceeds bounds, the input border flashes red and the Save button is disabled.
  • Cross-Paper Shield: Candidates are matched strictly by their unique candidate ID and class bundle. Generic script numbers (e.g. SC-001) from P5 can never overwrite SC-001 in P3.

4. Physical Script Verification Gate (Zero Wrong-Candidate Scoring)

Because markers pick physical booklets randomly from the class stack, the system implements a mandatory Booklet Verification Gate on every unmarked script:

⚠️ Mandatory Verification Step Before Marking Can Begin:
  1. Select the class bundle matching your physical stack (e.g. Humility 1 / 4.1).
  2. Search or click the candidate card corresponding to the booklet in front of you.
  3. Input Shielding Active: Notice that mark inputs, feedback tags, and the Save button are initially shielded and locked (dimmed at 40% opacity).
  4. Read the amber verification banner: "⚠️ Verify Physical Script Booklet Cover: Does this digital candidate match the physical paper booklet in your hand?"
  5. Check the printed Candidate Name, Register Number, and Class on the booklet cover against the screen.
  6. Click the green confirmation button: [✓ Yes, Booklet Matches Candidate (Unlock Marking)].
  7. The banner turns green (✓ Verified Candidate Booklet) and your cursor automatically jumps to the Content Mark field.

This protocol ensures 100% compliance with examination standards and prevents accidental entry into the wrong pupil's record.

5. Scoring & Qualitative Feedback Taxonomy

5.1 Entering Scores

  • Content Mark: Enter marks awarded for plot development, idea elaboration, and theme relevance (P3/P4: 0–7; P5: 0–20).
  • Language Mark: Enter marks awarded for vocabulary, sentence variation, paragraphing, and mechanics (P3/P4: 0–8; P5: 0–20).
  • Instant Sum & Percentage: The total mark and percentage are calculated dynamically upon every keystroke.

5.2 Tagging Feedback Descriptors

Feedback descriptors are categorized into Writing Strengths and Diagnostic Gaps:

  • 🟢 Green Descriptors (写作亮点与优势 / Writing Strengths): Commendable techniques and creative merit.
  • 🔴 Pink Descriptors (常见失分点与短板 / Common Gaps): Diagnostic weaknesses for subsequent classroom remediation.

6. Pre-Advance Score Confirmation & 10-Minute Amendment Window

6.1 Pre-Advance Score Confirmation Modal

When you click 💾 Save & Confirm Score (or press Enter):

  • A high-contrast confirmation modal appears displaying the candidate's name, class, Script ID, Content mark, Language mark, and total score.
  • Verify the score on screen against the hardcopy marks on the script cover.
  • Press Enter (↵) or click Confirm & Save Score.
  • The score is securely recorded in local storage and synced to the shared drive.
  • The system returns you directly to the class candidate picker so you can draw the next random booklet from your physical stack.

6.2 The 10-Minute Amendment Window

⏱️ 10-Minute Grace Period Protocol:
  • Upon saving a score, a 10-minute countdown timer starts for that candidate.
  • During this 10-minute window, you may reopen the script and amend marks, tags, or remarks freely if you notice a recalculation error.
  • Once the 10-minute window expires, the script transitions to Finalized & Locked status to protect standardisation integrity.
  • If any adjustment is required after finalization, please consult your Key Personnel (KP) Moderator or HOD, who hold authorization to moderate finalized scripts.

6.3 Key Personnel (KP) Moderation & Sampling Hub

If you are assigned as a Key Personnel (KP) Moderator:

  • During login, enter your confidential KP Authorization PIN (e.g. MOD1132).
  • In the Marker Portal, click the gold banner ⭐ Open KP Moderation & Script Sampling Hub.
  • Use the standardisation sampling filters to inspect borderline scripts, quality passes, and mark discrepancies across the cohort.
  • You can record moderated scores with full audit justification trails while preserving original marker marks intact.
